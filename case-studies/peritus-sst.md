# Peritus SST

### AI-assisted occupational safety report generation

A local-first workflow for turning field evidence, documents and technical inputs into structured occupational safety reports.

## How I approached it

I structured document extraction, information review and report generation as separate stages. OCR and optional AI support remain subject to professional review.
